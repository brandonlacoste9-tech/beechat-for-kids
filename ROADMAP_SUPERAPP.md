# 🚀 OuiChat Superapp Roadmap (Saved for Later)

> Strategy: Win one daily habit (chat + local utility), then expand to superapp status.

---

## What Makes WeChat "Super"

WeChat's core is messaging, wrapped by:
- **Moments** - social sharing
- **WeChat Pay** - QR payments
- **Mini Programs** - run inside the app without separate downloads
- Users can browse, book, shop, register, and get support in one place

---

## The Québec Superapp Wedge (Build First)

Pick one "must-open daily" wedge, then expand:

### 1. Local Chat-First Services
- Business inbox for SMBs (restaurants, barbers, dentists)
- Appointment booking
- Customer support threads (chat as CRM-lite)

### 2. "Mini-apps" for Québec Life
- Reservations
- Takeout ordering
- Loyalty/membership cards
- Event tickets
- Classifieds
- Local deals

### 3. QR Everywhere
- Québec loves in-person commerce
- QR codes bridge: physical → chat → booking/order → receipt
- (Mirrors WeChat Pay QR-based transactions)

---

## Payments: Superpower but Regulated

**⚠️ Canada MSB (Money Services Business) Obligations:**

If you add wallet-like features:
- P2P transfers
- Bill-pay
- Storing balances
- Money transmission
- Invoice payment services

**Requirements:**
- Register with FINTRAC
- KYC/identity verification
- Reporting and record keeping
- Compliance elements

**Recommendation:** Partner with regulated provider OR plan MSB compliance early.

## Platform Strategy ("Mini-Programs" for Québec)

### Business "Official Accounts"
- Verified merchant profiles
- Chat + posts/updates
- Promos and support flows
- Major business interface (like WeChat)

### Mini-App Framework
- Partners ship small experiences inside OuiChat
- Booking, ordering, forms, membership, support
- No app store installs required
- Core "superapp" mechanic

### AI Concierge
- Route users from intent to right mini-app
- Example: "book a haircut in Pointe-Claire tomorrow"
- Structured journeys with AI-enabled chat

## 90-Day Build Plan

### Weeks 1-3: Foundation
- Ship solid messaging

### Weeks 4-8: Mini-App Runtime
- Webview + auth + permissions + deep links
- 3 mini-app templates:
  1. Booking
  2. Menu/order
  3. Loyalty

### Weeks 9-12: Payments & QR
- QR codes
- Receipts
- **Option A:** Payment partner integration (faster)
